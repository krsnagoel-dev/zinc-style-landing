Flair / separate concept preview

One shopping decision.
Five useful moments.

The proposed theme is a quiet decision thread. Each card shows a real step in an in-app shopping flow instead of an unrelated stock photo. Play each short motion below. Nothing here changes the live five-feature section. On desktop, a single active subhead follows a progress rail; when the bar completes, the next subhead replaces it.

Illustrative interface only. No real offers, payment, order, earnings amount or merchant confirmation. Motion runs only when you press Play; it pauses when you leave the tab, and reduced-motion settings show the final static state.

The desktop progress handoff

Press Play to see the five cards advance. Each bar fills before the next card starts. The previous subhead collapses on handoff, and only the current subhead is visible. Pause or step manually.
