Flair / separate feature concept

Five features, one connected shopping flow.

The existing five feature headlines and subheads are kept word for word below. These scenes are generic capabilities, not app-specific examples. The light surfaces, product photos, thin borders and glass-like spacing follow the live Flair hero design. These are illustrative motion ideas, not a claim that a checkout, offer or partner integration is live.

Each scene is a separate short, non-looping study. On desktop, the progress rail fills one bar before the next feature; the old subhead collapses so only the current one appears. Play, pause or step through. Reduced-motion settings keep the scenes static.
